🌍 Regional Renewals by Product EMEA · NA · APJ
Renewal Rate — Web Experimentation by Region (Q1 vs Q2 2026)
Key insight: EMEA Web Experimentation recovered sharply from 58.9% in Q1 to 82.8% in Q2 — a +23.9pp swing. NA and APJ also improved materially. The Q1 EMEA trough appears to have been driven by a concentrated cohort of large accounts churning (see raw data); the Q2 cohort is smaller but higher-quality.
Renewal Rate — Feature Experimentation by Region (Q1 vs Q2 2026)
Key insight: Feature Experimentation mirrors the WebEx recovery. EMEA FX jumped from 56.6% to 89.1%, and NA from 71.9% to 87.6%. APJ hit 100% in Q2 (5 accounts, no churn). The EMEA improvement is the standout — worth investigating whether specific retention plays or CSM interventions drove this.
ARR at Renewal by Product & Region — Q1 vs Q2 Comparison
Key insight: NA Web Experimentation carries the largest absolute ARR ($11.3M in Q1, $9.8M in Q2), making it the single most important cohort for EO renewal health. A 1pp improvement in NA WebEx renewal is worth ~$113k in retained ARR.
🔮 Opal Attach Correlation ATTACH LIFT
EO Renewal Rate: Opal Attached vs Not Attached (Q1 & Q2 2026)
Key insight: Opal-attached EO accounts renew at a dramatically higher rate — 88.9% (Q1) and 99.9% (Q2) vs 66.4% and 86.2% for non-attached accounts. The lift is +22pp in Q1 and +14pp in Q2. With only 5–6 Opal-attached EO accounts (2.1% attach rate), this represents a massive expansion opportunity: attaching Opal is a strong predictor of retention.
