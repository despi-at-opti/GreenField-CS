## Plan: Simplify 01* queries into 2 base queries

Replace the 5 existing files (`01a` through `01e`) with **2 new SQL files** that return row-level data. All aggregation, comparisons (QoQ/YoY), and cross-dimension pivots happen downstream in Python/pandas — the SQL just returns enriched, deduplicated base datasets.

---

### File 1: `01_base_customer.sql`
**Source**: `EXEC_DECK_RENEWAL_RATES_CUSTOMER` + joins to CDR and ARR bands

**Grain**: One row per `MASTER_CUSTOMER_ID × DATE_YQ`

**Scope**: 9 quarters back from `:target_quarter`

**Columns returned**:
- From exec_deck: `MASTER_CUSTOMER_ID`, `ACCOUNT_NAME`, `DATE_YQ`, `DATE_YM`, `ARR_RENEWAL`, `ARR_CHURN`, `REGION`, `TERRITORY`, `SFDC_SEGMENT`, `CSM`
- From CDR join: `CSM_MANAGER_NAME`, `CS_TEAM` (derived: Digital CSM / Named CSM)
- Derived: `REVENUE_SEGMENT` (Enterprise ≥$250k / Mid-Market ≥$100k / Digital <$100k — from SST total ARR)
- From ARR bands join: `TOTAL_ACCOUNT_ARR`, `ARR_BAND` (team-specific bins from `00_account_arr_bands` logic via SST)

**Supports**: current-quarter scorecards, QoQ/YoY comparisons, 9Q trend, breakdowns by region, territory, ARR band, SFDC segment, revenue segment, CS team, CSM manager.

---

### File 2: `01_base_product_group.sql`
**Source**: `EXEC_DECK_RENEWAL_RATES_PRODUCT_GROUP` + joins to CDR and ARR bands

**Grain**: One row per `MASTER_CUSTOMER_ID × PRODUCT_GROUP_CLEANED × DATE_YQ` (deduplicated)

**Scope**: 9 quarters back from `:target_quarter`

**Columns returned**:
- From exec_deck: `MASTER_CUSTOMER_ID`, `ACCOUNT_NAME`, `DATE_YQ`, `DATE_YM`, `PRODUCT_GROUP_CLEANED`, `ARR_RENEWAL`, `ARR_CHURN`, `REGION`, `TERRITORY`, `SFDC_SEGMENT`, `CSM`
- From CDR join: `CSM_MANAGER_NAME`, `CS_TEAM`
- Derived in SQL:
  - `PRODUCT_SOLUTION` (EO/EC/AI/CMP/EOL/Other/Services)
  - `PRODUCT_LINE` (Analytics/Experiment/Personalization/CMS/CMP/Recommendations/Commerce Connect/Opal)
  - `PRODUCT_TYPE` (Core/Non-Core — 15 defined core products)
  - `SOLUTION_TYPE` (Single/Multi — count of distinct non-NULL PRODUCT_SOLUTION per MCID×DATE_YQ)
- From ARR bands join: `TOTAL_ACCOUNT_ARR`, `ARR_BAND`, `REVENUE_SEGMENT`

**Supports**: all product/solution splits, cross-dimensions (solution type × product solution, × product group, × SFDC segment, × ARR band, × region; product line × solution type/region/ARR band; product group × solution type/region/ARR band), with filters on CS team, CSM manager, region, territory.
