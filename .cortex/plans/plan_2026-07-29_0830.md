## Implementation Steps

### 1. Fix product_category in 13_onboarding_metrics.sql (Queries 2 & 3)

Replace the old manual CASE at lines 214-229 (Query 2) and 395-409 (Query 3) with:
```sql
CASE
    WHEN prod.REPORTING_CATEGORY_C IN ('Web Experimentation', 'Feature Experimentation',
         'Personalization', 'Segmentation', 'Full Platform Experimentation')
         OR prod.REPORTING_CATEGORY_C ILIKE '%Analytics'
         THEN 'Experimentation'
    WHEN prod.REPORTING_CATEGORY_C = 'Opal' THEN 'Opal'
    WHEN prod.REPORTING_CATEGORY_C ILIKE '%CMS%' THEN 'CMS'
    ELSE 'Other Products'
END AS product_category
```
Also update the filter at line 361 from `!= 'Other'` to `!= 'Other Products'` and at line 443 same.

### 3. Rethink cohort analysis: Go-live quarter cohorts

Replace the TTGL-bucket retention table with a **go-live quarter cohort** approach:

**Query 3 rewrite** (`14_onboarding_renewal.sql`):
- Cohort = go-live quarter (YYYY-QN) — e.g., 2024-Q1, 2024-Q2, ...
- Base = accounts that went live in that quarter
- Eligible = accounts whose go-live quarter is ≥12 months ago
