## Context
- `FCT_SFDC_CHURN_SIGNALS` has one row per opportunity, with the SFDC reason fields, all `AIGEN_*` fields (including `AIGEN_PRODUCTS` and `AIGEN_TRIAL_*`), and dates.
- It has no region, CS team or CSM columns, and no renewal ARR. Those come from `FCT_CHURN`: account × quarter grain, renewal / full churn / downgrade ARR, with the owner and manager at quarter start. Region and CS team come from `DIM_ACCOUNT_QE`.
- Scope: churned or downgraded accounts from 2025-Q4 to 2026-Q4, the window where the AI read and the SFDC fields are usable. Before 2026 the SFDC fields carry a "low fill" caveat.

## Implementation steps
1. **New view `RPT_CHURN_ACCOUNT_HISTORY`** (`sql/marts/rpt_churn_account_history.sql`), at account × renewal quarter grain:
   - From `FCT_CHURN`: renewal ARR, churn ARR, downgrade ARR, Lost / Downgrade label, region, CS team, CSM manager, CSM, and the product list.
   - Rolled up from the signals view:
     - SFDC category and type: the value on the largest opp, plus a distinct list;
     - competitor or in-house: the distinct names and kinds;
     - earliest first-signal date, and days from it to the renewal date;
     - AI reasons;
     - trial product, sentiment and evidence.
   - **Chronological history:** an ordered array of `{date, type (signal / evidence / trial / summary), text, opp}` built from `AIGEN_FIRST_SIGNAL(_DATE)`, `AIGEN_REASON_EVIDENCE(_DATE)`, `AIGEN_TRIAL_EVIDENCE` and `AIGEN_SUMMARY`, sorted by date.
2. **Companion view `RPT_CHURN_COMPETITOR`:** competitor × quarter × region × CS team × AI product, with accounts lost and churn ARR. The flexible filters slice this view.
3. **Export script `scripts/build_churn_leadership.py`:** queries both views, embeds the data as JSON, and writes `releases/2026-Q4/churn_leadership_summary.html`, following the `html-authoring` rules (no inline handlers, no CDN, `addEventListener` in a single script block). Sections:
   - **A. Headline findings:** the four points shared earlier (competitors, reason quality, early warning, Opal as a save lever), with caveats.
   - **B. QoQ overview:** by quarter, accounts lost or downgraded, renewal ARR, churn ARR, downgrade ARR, and churn as a % of renewal. Split by SFDC churn category.
   - **C. Churn-save attempts:** accounts with an AI, Opal or Mark trial, showing sentiment, outcome (lost or downgraded) and evidence, sorted by churn ARR.
   - **D. Competitors:**
     - conclusions text;
     - filter dropdowns for CS team, region and AI product;
     - a pivot table: competitor | accounts lost | churn ARR, per quarter plus a total.
   - **E. Drill-down table:**
     - expandable rows: Region > CSM manager > CSM > account, with subtotals for renewal, churn and downgrade ARR at each level;
     - each account row: product list, renewal quarter, ARR renewal / churn / downgrade, Lost / Downgrade;
     - expanding an account shows: SFDC category and type, days since first risk signal, risk signal, churn reasons, evidence, competitor or in-house, trial info, and the chronological note history.
4. Update `RECOMMENDATIONS.md` and commit the SQL and the script (`releases/` stays ignored).
