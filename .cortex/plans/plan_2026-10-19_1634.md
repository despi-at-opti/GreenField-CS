## Method (per quarter-end E, from 2025-Q1 to the target quarter)

| Element | Window / point |
|---|---|
| Population and baseline | WebX + FX ARR > 0 in `ARR_REPORT` at the opening snapshot E−12mo |
| Churn | `ADAPTIVE_MOVEMENTS_DATA`, Product Group Split Bridge, WebX/FX product groups, snapshot date in (E−12mo, E] |
| Experiments and flags | **(E−24mo, E−12mo]**: the 12 months before the opening snapshot, no churn cap |
| Usage tier, dark flag, stat-sig rate, creators, cost per experiment | from that leading window |
| ARR band and product type | at the opening snapshot |
| Tenure flag | `NEW_ACCOUNT` = account's first WebX/FX snapshot is less than 12 months before opening; reported as its own group, not as Dark |
| Closing status | closing WebX/FX ARR at E: Full Churn (= 0), Downgrade (net loss, ARR remains), Retained |

- **Churn buckets (same as 05a):**
  - Full (logo) churn: `Churn`, `Churn_swap`, `Winback`, `Lapsed Renewal`
  - Downgrade: `Downgrade*`, `Downsell*`, `Win back Downgrade/Downsell`
  - Total churn = full churn + downgrade
- **Retention rate** = (baseline + total churn) ÷ baseline. Churn is counted once.
- **Overlapping windows:** quarterly windows share 9 of their 12 months, so the trend is a trailing view, the same as GDR.
